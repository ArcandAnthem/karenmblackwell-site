Current pathways

Ways to work together

The strongest partnerships are specific, reciprocal, and grounded in a shared research or public-impact objective.

01Grant development

Interdisciplinary proposals connecting consumer research with healthcare, patient experience, health services, measurement, or public policy.

02Data and populations

Appropriate de-identified datasets, participant populations, or healthcare settings that can support rigorous research under required approvals.

03Research and coauthorship

Complementary substantive or methodological expertise, including health services, patient-reported measurement, psychometrics, healthcare quality, and policy.

04Presentations and scholarly exchange

Invited talks, research workshops, panels, academic conversations, and responsible public translation of emerging work.

05Faculty opportunities

Marketing departments seeking an educator and researcher who brings healthcare, executive, entrepreneurial, and inclusive-teaching experience.

06Selected professional practice

Research-informed engagements in market research, strategy, positioning, customer insight, and AI-supported marketing.

A good partnership fit

Clear contribution, respectful process, shared value

I am especially interested in collaborators who value careful construct definition, responsible measurement, interdisciplinary learning, transparent expectations, and practical relevance without overstating preliminary evidence.

  • A defined research, grant, data, or presentation opportunity
  • Complementary expertise or access
  • Respect for institutional approvals and participant protections
  • A realistic path to shared scholarly or public value
