Teaching

Applied, inclusive, and research-informed marketing education

My teaching connects marketing theory to real decisions while creating clear, welcoming, and appropriately challenging learning environments.

Teaching philosophy

Students learn best when relevance and structure work together

I combine inclusive learning, active application, research literacy, transparent design, and ethical AI integration so students can understand marketing concepts and use them with judgment.

Inclusive learning

Welcoming participation, transparent expectations, multiple ways to engage, and respect for varied student experiences.

Applied instruction

Current brands, market research, simulations, role plays, guest speakers, and projects tied to professional decision-making.

Research-informed design

Activities and assessments aligned with learning outcomes, supported by evidence-based teaching practices.

Ethical AI integration

Helping students use AI thoughtfully for inquiry, analysis, communication, and responsible marketing practice.

Course quality and design

Designing learning experiences that are clear and accessible

My preparation includes the ACUE Certificate in Effective College Instruction and formal online-course development using Quality Matters standards.

MKT 3210: Personal Selling was redesigned through Cal State LA’s Online Course Development Program using the Quality Matters Higher Education Rubric, Seventh Edition. After the amendment process, the course met expectations in a QM-Managed Preparatory Review conducted by a certified Quality Matters Master Reviewer.

Core design practices

  • Alignment among objectives, activities, and assessments
  • Accessible Canvas organization and learner support
  • Transparent instructions and milestone-based projects
  • Active learning and real-world application
  • Ethical and purposeful use of AI
