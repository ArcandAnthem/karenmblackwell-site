About Karen

Research shaped by healthcare leadership, education, and practice

Karen M. Blackwell is a marketing researcher, university educator, and former healthcare executive whose work examines consumer behavior across the healthcare ecosystem. As a doctoral researcher in marketing at Virginia Tech’s Pamplin College of Business, she studies healthcare as a consumer domain encompassing interactions with professionals, organizations, information, digital tools, and emerging technologies.

Her interdisciplinary research interests include patient experience, healthcare decision-making, digital health, AI-enabled health information, healthcare systems, and marketing and public policy. Her scholarship is informed by executive leadership experience in healthcare marketing, strategy, market access, commercialization, and organizational growth.

Karen has also founded and led a research-informed marketing practice, translating consumer insight, data, and strategy into practical organizational decisions. Across multiple California State University campuses, she connects marketing theory with applied decision-making, inclusive course design, and students’ professional development.
